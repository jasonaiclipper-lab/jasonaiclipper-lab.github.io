This policy covers the Replay Raid website and Replay Raid Uploader, the internal tool we use to publish videos to our own YouTube channel and TikTok account.

What we collect

The website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking, and does not collect personal information.

Replay Raid Uploader only signs in to accounts owned by Replay Raid. It does not collect data about viewers or any other users. For our own accounts it stores and uses:

  • Sign-in tokens from Google/YouTube and TikTok, kept on our own computer so the tool can publish our videos.
  • Basic TikTok profile information (user.info.basic): account ID, display name and avatar, shown on the settings page so the owner can confirm which account is posting.
  • TikTok posting information returned by the Content Posting API: the account's available privacy options, whether comments, duets and stitches are allowed, the maximum video length, and the status and ID of each video we publish.
  • Posting settings chosen by the account owner (privacy level, interaction settings, content disclosure and the date they agreed to TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation).
  • View counts for our own published videos.

How we use it

This data is used only to upload, publish, update and track the performance of Replay Raid's own videos: video.upload sends videos to our TikTok inbox as drafts, and video.publish posts videos directly to our TikTok profile using the settings the account owner chose. We do not sell, share or transfer this data to anyone, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Third-party services

Replay Raid Uploader uses YouTube API Services and the TikTok Content Posting API. By using our content you are also subject to the Google Privacy Policy, the YouTube Terms of Service and TikTok's Privacy Policy. Access granted to our tool can be revoked at any time via Google security settings or the TikTok app's settings.

Retention and deletion

Sign-in tokens and posting settings are stored only on our own computer, kept only while the tool is in use, and deleted when access is revoked. Downloaded and rendered video files are deleted automatically within a few days. To request deletion of any data, email the address below.

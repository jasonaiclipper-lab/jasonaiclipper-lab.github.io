Replay Raid publishes short vertical videos of the most-watched moments from Twitch gaming streams, starting with Grand Theft Auto VI. Every video adds captions and a hook, and credits the streamer on screen and in the description with a link to their channel.

How it works

Replay Raid Uploader is our own internal tool. It finds popular viewer-made Twitch clips, edits them into vertical videos, and publishes them to our own YouTube channel and TikTok account using the official YouTube Data API and TikTok Content Posting API. The account owner chooses the TikTok posting settings (who can view, comments, duets, stitches and content disclosure) on the tool's settings page, and can pause automatic posting at any time. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's accounts.

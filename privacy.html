This policy covers the Replay Raid website and Replay Raid Uploader, the internal tool we use to publish videos to our own YouTube channel and TikTok account.

What we collect

The website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking, and does not collect personal information. Replay Raid Uploader only signs in to accounts owned by Replay Raid. It stores the sign-in tokens for those accounts (from Google/YouTube and TikTok) on our own computer so it can upload our videos. It reads basic profile information (account name and ID) and view counts for our own videos. It does not collect data about viewers or any other users.

How we use it

Tokens and account data are used only to upload, update and track the performance of Replay Raid's own videos. We do not sell, share or transfer this data to anyone.

Third-party services

Replay Raid Uploader uses YouTube API Services and the TikTok Content Posting API. By using our content you are also subject to the Google Privacy Policy, the YouTube Terms of Service and TikTok's Privacy Policy. Access granted to our tool can be revoked at any time via Google security settings or the TikTok app's settings.

Retention and deletion

Sign-in tokens are kept only while the tool is in use and are deleted when access is revoked. Downloaded clip files are deleted automatically within a few days.

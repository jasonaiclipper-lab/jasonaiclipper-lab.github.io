About

Replay Raid publishes short clips of public Twitch streams with commentary-style captions, hooks and credit to the original streamer. Replay Raid Uploader is an internal tool used only by Replay Raid to publish to its own accounts; it is not available to the public.

Publishing to TikTok

Replay Raid Uploader publishes videos only to Replay Raid's own TikTok account, using the TikTok Content Posting API. Before any automatic posting, the account owner confirms the account, chooses who can view the videos, chooses whether comments, duets and stitches are allowed, declares any promotional or branded content, and agrees to TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation (and, for branded content, the Branded Content Policy). These settings can be changed, and automatic posting paused, at any time. Videos from paid clipping campaigns are disclosed as branded content.

Content and credit

All gameplay and stream content belongs to its respective owners. Every Replay Raid video names and links the original streamer. We do not claim ownership of streamers' content.

Takedown requests

If you own content that appears in one of our videos and want it removed, comment on the video or email jasonaiclipper@gmail.com. We will remove it promptly and stop clipping your channel.
